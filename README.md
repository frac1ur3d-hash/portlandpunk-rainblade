## ⚡ What is Portland Protocol: Rain Blade?

A fast-paced, high-octane 8-Bit Cyber Hack & Slash ARPG set across rain-soaked Pacific Northwest anomaly zones. Deflect incoming bullets back at rogue street bosses, unlock Diablo 3-style skill rune augments, level up your weapons through active combat, and scavenge over 120+ Portland-themed weapons, coats, and urban relics.

---

## 🔥 Key Gameplay Features

### 1. ⚔️ Tactical Blade Ricochet Physics
Slashing through incoming ranged bullets calculates your blade's contact angle. Hostile bullets are converted into player-owned neon cyan (`#00f5ff`) projectiles with **+55% velocity boost** and sent flying back at nearby enemies for **critical ricochet damage**.

### 2. 🔮 Diablo 3-Style Skill Tree & Runes (`K`)
Customize 4 distinct disciplines with 3 selectable runes each that transform elemental damage, sound fx, and particle effects:
- **Atmospheric Rain Cleave** (*Pacific Tsunami*, *Lightning Leyline*, *Cryo Hailstorm*)
- **Cold Brew Hyper-Drive** (*Caffeine Overclock*, *Espresso Shockwave*, *Nitro Cold Foam Shield*)
- **Rose City Briar Cyclone** (*Iron Thorns*, *Portlandia Petal Gale*, *Vortex of the Rose God*)
- **Forest Park Symbiosis** (*Deep Root Cellular Regen*, *Barkskin Aegis*, *Pacific Chlorophyll Surge*)

### 3. 🛡️ Gear Experience & Reforging (`I`)
Equipped weapons, armors, and relics accumulate **Weapon XP** as you slay street horrors. When an item reaches max mastery, spend Stumptown Beans to **Reforge** it:
- Grants **+25% stat boosts** per rank
- Promotes gear star tiers up to **★★★★★ / Level 10**

### 4. 🗺️ 120+ Diverse Portland-Themed Items
- **40 Weapons**: *Powell's Tome Blade*, *Steel Bridge Chainsaw*, *Hawthorne Neon Katana*, *St. Johns Spire Rapier*, *Portlandia Trance Trident*
- **40 Armors**: *Pearl Nanoweave Suit*, *Waxed Canvas Duster*, *OMSI Faraday Cage Trench*, *Chinatown Dragon Silk Robe*
- **40 Relics**: *Voodoo Bacon Maple Donut Ring*, *MAX Transit Pass of Phasing*, *Timbers Championship Axe Head*, *Rose Festival Emerald Rosette*
